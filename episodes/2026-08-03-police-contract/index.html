Mpls Cops Negotiate Contract in Private Mediation

August 3, 2026

Did you know that city and police representatives are negotiating another three year contract right now? Police are asking for significant pay premiums on top of the historic raise they won in the prior contract. Meanwhile Councilmember Robin Wonsley put out a list of proposals she wants to see in the contract. We talk through those, status of negotiations, and the challenge of trying to use the police contract as an instrument of positive change.

Minneapolis For a Better Police Contract

This advocacy group (website) was formed by members who included the Racial Justice Network and Communities United Against Police Brutality. They initially put out 19 recommendations for changes to the contract way back in 2019. They have also sought to win the support of city councilmembers for those proposals. Unfortunately, they were not enacted. In 2022, those were expanded to 22 recommendations, and ultimately four councilmembers voted against the 2023-2025 contract.

In our conversation, we referred to this group as pretty tightly associated with Michelle Gross/Communities United Against Police Brutaility. Stacey Gurian-Sherman, a steering committee member for MFBPC, helped clarify that this has always been an independent group, not a project "of" CUAPB. Gross and Dave Bicking had left MFBPC earlier than we indicated in our conversation.

2023-2025 Contract

  • 21% pay raise
  • Federation approved in 301-63 vote
  • Sept 2023: negotiations started
  • July 2024, city council approves new contract

    • Officers will no longer receive automatic notification of the identity of the person requesting public personnel data about them.
    • When there are allegations of severe misconduct, the Chief will have complete discretion to place the officer on paid investigatory leave for up to 180 days. In the past, the Chief only had complete discretion for the first 30 days.
