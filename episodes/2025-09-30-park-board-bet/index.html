Big Change Ahead for the Park Board, and the BET Has Real Policy Disputes?

September 30, 2025

Two less-visible boards are up for election in November, and there's a lot more change brewing than you might think. The Park Board could see a dramatic change in composition, with a lot of labor- and youth-fueled energy in a bunch of races. Meanwhile the Board of Estimate and Taxation has on the table an actual policy idea of substance and contention. We walk through the key races and candidates, and introduce where both of these boards sit in city governance and history.

We recorded this episode on 9/24/25.
