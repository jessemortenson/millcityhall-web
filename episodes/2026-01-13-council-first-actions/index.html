Fiery First Council Meeting Lays Strategy Cards on the Table

January 13, 2026

Council President Payne played his hand: elected a new vice president, returned a version of council majority and council minority leader positions, and enlarged committees. Democratic Socialists have a formal role in leadership. And the Frey camp reacted with open anger. We discuss what this reveals about the theory of change in city hall.

Note: while this episode was being recorded, Renee Nicole Macklin Good was murdered by federal agent Johnathan Ross. We heard the horrific news after we wrapped. Please plug in via the links below, and join us in the streets.

Recorded 1/7/26.
