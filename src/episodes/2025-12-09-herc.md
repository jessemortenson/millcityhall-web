With a [Dec 18 town hall coming up](https://www.instagram.com/p/DRx0LZuAa-Z/), the Zero Burn Coalition is building momentum to close the HERC (Hennepin Energy Recovery Center), a garbage burner that burns almost half of our trash and pollutes the air. Consensus is emerging to shutter this source of environmental injustice, but county staff have pushed back against real deadlines. Our own Cam Gordon is a member of the Coalition, and he and Robin Garwood worked on zero waste plans for the city. 

The Zero Burn Coalition town hall is scheduled for:

> [Dec 18, 5:30pm](https://www.instagram.com/p/DRx0LZuAa-Z/)<br />
> Urban League Twin Cities<br />
> 2100 Plymouth Ave N<br />
> Minneapolis

What will it take to close the garbage burner? What do you do as an activist when your elected official allies aren't getting results as fast as you want? How did we come to a place where at least three levels of government are pointing fingers at each other?

Recorded 12/03/25.

## HERC: Hennepin County Energy Recovery Center

![HERC trash diversion goals](https://millcityhall-public.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/images/herc_diversion.png)

- [County staff presentation on history, and zero waste roadmap for closing HERC](https://www.hennepin.us/-/media/hennepinus/your-government/projects-initiatives/solid-waste-planning/reinventing-solid-waste-system-presentation.pdf): pretty informative context and history here
- County staff priorities/roadmap items per presentation:
  - Prioritize extended producer responsibility (Legislature) 
  - Secure adequate funding for zero-waste initiatives through SCORE and other sources (Legislature) 
  - Ban recyclable and organic materials from landfills (Legislature and MPCA) 
  - Recover recyclable materials from the trash – recycling recovery facility (Legislature and county)
  - Support the transition to organized collection across Hennepin County (Legislature, county and cities) 
  - Increase compliance with Ordinance 13 and expand requirements (County) 
  - Develop and implement county plan to eliminate food waste (Legislature and county)
  - Expand collection and drop-off options for hard to recycle items (Legislature and county)
  - Reduce single-use plastics and plastic packaging (Legislature and producers) Increase the reuse and recycling of construction and demolition waste (Legislature) 
  - Mandate participation in recycling and composting programs (Legislature and county) 
  - Ensure every individual has equitable access to zero-waste tools (Legislature and county

## Selected Timeline

![HERC timeline graphic](https://millcityhall-public.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/images/herc_waste_mgmt_history.png)

- **1989** HERC opens
- **Sep 21, 2023** Staff recommend "a closure date for the HERC in the estimated timeframe of 2040 - 2050"
- **Oct 24, 2023** Henn Co Commission adopts resolution "to develop a plan for the closure of the HERC ... between 2028 and 2040" [source](https://hennepinmn.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=6369979&GUID=84407533-7A2C-4172-92AD-D41195895CCE&Options=ID%7cText%7c&Search=HERC&emci=ef90080c-3bca-f011-8196-6045bdfe8e9c&emdi=eb44e3a8-00cb-f011-8195-000d3a1d58aa&ceid=2911864)
- **Jan 25, 2024** Staff brief Henn Co board wtih plans [source](https://www.hennepin.us/-/media/hennepinus/your-government/projects-initiatives/solid-waste-planning/reinventing-solid-waste-system-presentation.pdf)
- **Sep 11, 2024** Randy Kiser, operations and compliance manager for Hennepin County’s solid waste and energy division, emailed Xcel Energy officials in September 2024 to assure them the HERC would remain open for the foreseeable future [source](https://sahanjournal.com/climate-environment/herc-closure-plan-frustration-hennepin-county/)

> “Hennepin County will continue to operate HERC into the future with no plans to terminate operation,” Kiser wrote on Sept.11, 2024. 

- **Sep 1, 2024** Irene Fernano asked the Minneapolis City Council ... to chip in with a plan to manage waste without the incinerator. [source](https://sahanjournal.com/climate-environment/herc-closure-plan-frustration-hennepin-county/)
- **Oct 31, 2024** Mpls City Council passes resolution urging closure of HERC [source](https://sahanjournal.com/climate-environment/minneapolis-city-council-herc-resolution/)
