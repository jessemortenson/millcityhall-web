## Pointergate

We referenced the "Pointergate" controversy from 2014, when the Minneapolis police union targeted then-mayor Betsy Hodges with claims that she shared "gang signs" with a resident in a posed photo. These claims quickly fell apart and the story gained larger prominence as an absurd attempt at a political hit. KSTP, owned by the local rich and conservative Hubbard family, stuck by the report and its reporter, Jay Kolls.

* [MPR explainer](https://www.mprnews.org/story/2014/11/07/explaining-pointergate)
* [Columbia Journalism Review timeline](https://www.cjr.org/united_states_project/timeline_kstp_pointergate_defense.php)
