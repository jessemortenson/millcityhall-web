The Trump regime charged 15 Minnesotans with conspiracy, allegedly in connection to resistance to the ICE invasion. Emmett Doyle is one of the Minnesota 15, and he joined us to share his experiences and perspectives. This group of people is engaged in a long legal battle with uncertain - and potentially serious - consequences. We talked about what surprises have come out in discovery, the personal and community risks of this kind of repressive political prosecution, and what it takes to fight back.

Recorded 11/18/26.
