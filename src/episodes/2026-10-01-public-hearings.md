## Robin's Political Science References

- C. Wright Mills The Power Elite made the argument that elites rule national politics in 1956
- Robert Dahl’s Who governs?  from 1961. Studied New Haven, CT, and found that political power is “pluralist” in the US setting. Elites don’t rule, but neither do the masses. Political power is distributed, but unevenly.
- Bachrach and Baratz released an article in the American Political Science Review criticizing Dahl “for focusing on ‘the ability to initiate and veto proposals’ while ignoring the ability to prevent proposals from even being considered in the first place."
- Third face of power is from Steven Lukes in Power: A Radical View, which says that elites influence people’s preferences through more subtle and pervasive societal norms. We are controlled without knowing that’s what’s happening
